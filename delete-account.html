Delete Your Share eat! Account

Share eat! (com.tryshareeat.app on Google Play, also available on the App Store) — operated by Share Eat.

You can request deletion of your account and the personal data associated with it at any time. Every request is handled by our team so we can verify it and meet our legal obligations.

Request Account Deletion

Opens a pre-filled email to hello@shareeat.io — send it from the email address linked to your account so we can verify the request.

How it works

  • Tap the button above (or use Settings → Delete Account inside the app, which opens the same email), then send the email.
  • If the button doesn't open your mail app, simply email hello@shareeat.io from your account email with the subject "Account Deletion Request".
  • We action deletion requests within 21 calendar days of receiving them.

What gets deleted

Your account and the personal data associated with it — including your profile (name, photo, contact details), sign-in identifiers, and app content tied to your account.

What we may retain, and for how long

  • A permanent log of your privacy-policy consent (policy version, action, timestamp), kept as a legal record to evidence compliance with the PDPA.
  • Limited data where retention is required by law or needed to resolve an active dispute — kept no longer than necessary, and no longer than 12 months in the case of a suspended (rather than deleted) account.

Full details are in our Privacy Policy (Sections 9.2 and 9.3).

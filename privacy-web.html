Website Privacy Notice

Effective Date: 14 August 2026

1. Who We Are & What This Notice Covers

This notice explains what information is collected when you visit our website, shareeat.io, including The Harga Index pages at shareeat.io/harga. The website is operated by Share Eat Sdn. Bhd. ("we", "our", "us"), which is the data controller for the purposes of the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 of Malaysia (as amended, the "PDPA"). This document also serves as our Personal Data Protection Notice for website visitors, issued pursuant to Section 7 of the PDPA.

This notice covers the website only. The Share Eat mobile application has its own Privacy Policy, which governs everything you do inside the App.

2. Website Analytics (PostHog)

We use PostHog (PostHog, Inc., United States) to understand how the website is used. It collects usage events — the pages you view, and actions you take on the site such as starting or completing the Harga price-guessing game, sharing your result, or tapping a WhatsApp or app-store link — together with your IP address (from which approximate, city-level location may be derived), browser and device information, and a randomly generated identifier stored in your browser's local storage and cookies so that repeat visits from the same browser can be counted.

We do not attach your name, email address, or other directly identifying details to this data, and we use it only in aggregated form to understand and improve the website and to report anonymous usage statistics (for example, how many people played the Harga Challenge). PostHog processes this data on our behalf under its own privacy and data-processing terms.

3. Meta Pixel

Some pages of our website use the Meta Pixel (Meta Platforms, Inc., United States), a piece of code that sets cookies and collects information about your visit — the pages you view, actions you take on the site (for example, tapping a button to contact us on WhatsApp), your IP address, and browser and device information — and shares it with Meta. We use this to measure the effectiveness of our advertising and to understand how visitors reach our site. Meta processes this data under its own privacy policy and may associate it with your Facebook or Instagram account if you have one.

4. The Harga Challenge Game

The Harga price-guessing game runs entirely in your browser. Your individual guesses and scores are not linked to your identity; we receive only the anonymous analytics events described in Section 2 (for example, that a game was started or completed and the score achieved). If you choose to share your result, the share happens through your device's own share sheet — we do not see who you share it with.

5. Forms, Email & WhatsApp

Some pages include a waitlist or sign-up form hosted by Tally (Tally BV, Belgium). Information you enter into such a form (for example, your name, email address, phone number, or social media handles) is collected through Tally on our behalf and used only to contact you about Share Eat. Tally processes this data as our service provider under its own privacy and data-processing terms.

If you contact us by email or through a WhatsApp link on the website, we receive the contact details and message content you choose to send, and we use them to respond to your enquiry. WhatsApp is operated by Meta and is governed by its own terms and privacy policy.

6. Hosting & Security Logs

The website is served through GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States) and Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., United States). Like virtually all web hosts, these providers process your IP address and request information in server and security logs to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. They act as our service providers under their own privacy and data-processing terms.

7. Your Choices

  • You can block or limit all of the tracking described above by blocking or deleting cookies and site data in your browser settings, or by using a browser or extension that blocks trackers. The website, including the Harga game, works fully without them.
  • If you have a Meta account, you can adjust your advertising preferences in your Meta account settings (Settings → Ad preferences).
  • You may contact us at any time to ask what data we hold about you or to request its deletion (see Section 9).

8. International Transfers & Retention

The service providers named above store data on servers outside Malaysia, primarily in the United States and the European Union. We retain website analytics data only as long as needed for the aggregated reporting described in this notice, and information you send us directly only as long as needed to handle your enquiry, unless a longer period is required by law.

9. Changes & Contact

We may update this notice from time to time; the version and effective date at the top of this page will tell you when it last changed. For any privacy question or request, contact us at share.eat2026@gmail.com or via the WhatsApp links on this website.

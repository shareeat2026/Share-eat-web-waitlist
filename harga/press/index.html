📰The story in one paragraph

A Malaysian food-creator startup spent three days hand-counting what four everyday items cost on public delivery menus in every state, and published all 619 prices. The gap is wider than most people would guess: the cheapest nasi lemak with chicken they found was RM5.30 in Terengganu, the most expensive nasi lemak of any kind was RM40.00 in Putrajaya, a prawn set. Along the way they found that a median iced matcha now costs more than a median plate of nasi lemak, and that in Kelantan even the priciest nasi lemak on the platform costs less than the national median plate.

✅Worth getting right

  • The RM40.00 plate is a prawn set, not a plain nasi lemak. It is the most expensive nasi lemak of any kind in that state, which is a different claim.
  • These are delivery-platform prices, listed before discounts. Counter prices are usually lower.
  • State figures are medians, not averages.
  • It is a snapshot, 11 to 13 August 2026. Every row is dated.

🍽️About Share Eat!

Share Eat! is a Malaysian platform where restaurants offer a meal as food credit and local food creators apply to feature them. The restaurant pays in food rather than cash and keeps every video; the creator eats and films without paying out of pocket. It is live on the App Store and currently onboarding its founding hundred Klang Valley restaurants.

The Harga Index came out of that work: if you are asking creators to discover restaurants for a living, it helps to know what discovery actually costs.
